Grade 5 Skills Map

These strands mirror the topics taught in school. Work through them in order or mix things up to keep practice fresh.

Number Sense

  • Place value through millions and thousandths
  • Compare, order, and round decimals
  • Use expanded and word form fluently

Fractions

  • Add and subtract unlike denominators
  • Multiply fractions & mixed numbers
  • Relate fractions, decimals, and percents

Algebraic Thinking

  • Analyze patterns and sequences
  • Write and evaluate simple expressions
  • Solve two-step equations

Geometry & Measurement

  • Classify triangles & quadrilaterals
  • Calculate area, volume, and perimeter
  • Interpret coordinate planes
